# Editorial Style Wiki

## What works (keep doing)
- Open on the clearest verified development first; a single sourced move or status update can carry the story.
- Keep breaking-news updates concise and inside the one reported development instead of stretching for extra context.
- Make the headline and body match the same verified angle so the piece feels tightly grounded.
- Use specificity that is directly supported by the source; enough detail to inform, not enough to speculate.
- Stay narrow when the item is just one transaction or status note; disciplined focus reads cleaner than a broadened takeaway.
- Preserve trust by avoiding unsupported elaboration; clean grounding is a strength in insider updates.
- A simple reported-action story works when it is direct, specific, and unembellished.
- [insider] Report the single sourced development plainly; concise, factual delivery is the right tone.
- [columnist] A concrete camp-development angle can carry the piece when the reporting stays tightly source-bound.
- [columnist] Clean headline/intro alignment with a real training-camp update is enough; no extra framing needed.

## What to avoid
- Do not widen a one-move update into broader implications or team-building commentary unless the source supports it.
- Do not add unsupported details just to increase impact or make the story feel fuller.
- Do not let the headline imply more than the body actually verifies.
- Do not drift outside the single sourced development when the update is only a transaction/status note.
- Do not treat a concise breaking item as a cue to speculate about roster meaning or future outcomes.
- Do not replace direct reporting with generic context when the verified fact itself is the story.
- Do not bury the core news under extra setup; the main development should remain front and center.
- [insider] Do not overplay the angle with embellishment; keep the reporting crisp and source-bound.
- Do not infer additional personnel or decision-making details beyond what is explicitly reported.
- If the source digest has no usable facts, do not invent a narrative or specific status claim to salvage the piece (source-side, not fixable in prose).
