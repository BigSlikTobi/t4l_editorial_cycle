# Editorial Style Wiki

## What works (keep doing)
- Open on the actual status update or personnel call, with the key outcome clear in sentence one.
- Preserve the hedge when the source language is tentative; make uncertainty explicit instead of overstating certainty.
- Lead with the confirmed personnel update and the next practical implication for the room or plan. [insider]
- Surface the immediate backup or next-step consequence early when the source supports it.
- Keep the story anchored to the specific game, role, or assignment the source actually addresses.
- Use a tight lede that stays focused on the verified news rather than broadening into extra context.
- Keep headline and lede aligned on the same verified angle without copying the same phrasing too closely.
- Preserve clean, concise framing when the source package is straightforward and timely.
- Keep the body tightly grounded in the source digests; avoid meta language and unnecessary scene-setting.
- When the situation is stable enough for a clean approve, let the piece read as a direct, usable update rather than a generalized notebook item.

## What to avoid
- Do not add unsupported roster, camp, or background details that are not in the source package.
- Do not let unrelated practice notes or side references drift into a status update story.
- Do not bury the actual update under extra context when the source already gives a clear, usable angle.
- Do not widen a narrow availability report into a bigger narrative than the source supports.
- Do not treat fallback reps or contingency plans as interchangeable with confirmed lineup decisions.
- Do not overstate a players status when the source language is still conditional.
- Do not make headline, subhead, and lede repeat the same update without adding new source-grounded information.
- Keep the team/entity field matched to the storys real subject; a wrong-team mismatch can sink the piece even if the body is grounded.
- Avoid unsupported camp clutter; keep the opening tied to the verified update and contingency plan. [insider]
- Do not introduce the wrong personnel detail just to fill space; grounding matters more than added texture. [insider]
- Mark any source-side gap plainly in your own judgment, but do not try to paper over it with invented specifics.
