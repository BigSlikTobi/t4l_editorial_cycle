# Editorial Style Wiki

## What works (keep doing)
- Open on one verified news hook and keep the lede centered on that single development.
- Use a narrow, source-backed angle; a specific start, signing, or status update is stronger than a broadened take.
- Keep headline and lede tightly aligned so the same confirmed fact carries both.
- Thread in a few concrete, source-supported details to add density without widening the story.
- Preserve a direct, factual tone when the source supports a clean news brief.
- Keep the body focused on the announcement and the player’s verified background, not on broader roster interpretation.
- Tight factual framing can still be readable if every added detail is directly supported.
- [analyst] A quarterback-start or competition update works when it stays on the named decision and verified resume facts.
- [analyst] Drafts read well when they balance specificity with restraint: enough detail to show why the update matters, not enough to overreach.
- [analyst] Strong grounding matters most: use the source’s explicit call, draft slot, and production details without inventing connective tissue.
- [analyst] A team-specific reason to tap can lift the piece as long as it stays anchored to verified facts.

## What to avoid
- Do not add unsupported player, contract, or roster-detail references just to make the update feel fuller.
- Do not widen a simple announcement into broader competition, fallout, or roster-meaning language unless the digest supports it.
- Do not let the headline and lede drift beyond the specific verified event.
- Do not stack extra contextual claims that are not explicitly in the source set.
- Do not imply preseason or first-snap sequencing more strongly than the digest supports.
- Do not turn a clean personnel announcement into a multi-part roster narrative.
- Do not introduce contract-rework or other side details unless the digest explicitly includes them.
- Do not overstate what the announcement says about the quarterback race or job security.
- Do not treat source-adjacent background as if it were part of the core verified update.
- Do not rely on unsupported filler that competes with the main factual hook.
